## Squat
- Feet shoulder-width apart, toes slightly outward
- Hips back and down, heels flat on the floor
- Hip crease must drop below parallel with knees for full depth
- Watch for: knee valgus (knees caving in), butt wink (pelvic tilt at bottom)

## Push-up
- Rigid plank from head to heels, hands under shoulders
- Elbows track at 45 degrees — arrow shape, not a T
- Watch for: sagging hips, winging shoulder blades

## Lunge
- Step long enough for both knees to reach ~90 degrees
- Front knee stays over ankle, not past toes
- Torso upright, back knee hovers just off the floor
- Watch for: front heel lifting, torso collapsing forward
